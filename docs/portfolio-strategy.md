## 5. Information architecture

`/` curated Work homepage; `/research/` research agenda, projects and publications; `/about/` biography, capabilities, experience, education, recognition; `/archive/` filterable all-work index; `/life/` personal records; `/work/<slug>/` project case studies or briefs; `/handsign/index.html` standalone working demo. Resume always accessible at `/assets/JuminShin_Resume.pdf`.

Use ordinary semantic links and statically generated route HTML. Browser back, opening in a new tab, direct entry and refresh work without a state-only router. Retain React/Vite; generate static content and per-route metadata at build time. No hosting migration.

## 6. Homepage concept

An editorial opening with Jumin Shin as the dominant identity, human-centered AI positioning, and actual clinical-interface imagery as a wide work preview. Follow with five selected works in asymmetric image/typographic spreads. Work captions remain outside imagery. Production metrics are typographic data art, explicitly distinguished from original product UI. Research is a dark ink band with accessible paper entries; selected experience and a short about introduction lead to a direct email footer. Work, Research, About and Resume remain readily available.

## 7. Case-study system

Shared primitives: project header, metadata definition list, figure/caption, editorial section, research facts, workflow diagram, resource link, optional embed, gallery, next project. Each source-supported case supplies its own section titles. Supported observations and reflections are distinguished from measured findings. New diagrams must be captioned as retrospective summaries, not original interfaces or research artifacts. No invented quotes, methods, participants, adoption, impact, responsibilities or NDAs. Sparse resume-only sources receive polished briefs rather than speculative long case studies.

## 8-10. Visual identity, typography and color

Identity: careful human decisions inside complex technical systems. Use precise alignment, large type, figure numbering, open margins and a restrained red annotation mark. True near-neutral paper `#f6f6f3`, ink `#242521`, muted `#62635c`, divider `#d4d5ce`, vermilion `#b93624`, pale yellow `#efedac` and cool light gray `#e8edf0` for selected editorial project surfaces. No dominant green, purple or slate theme.

Typography: locally hosted open-source Geist Sans; system monospace for labels, with explicit fallback. Fixed breakpoint scales, never viewport-unit font sizes. Desktop display 112-144px, section heads 44-64px, case titles 64-88px; body 17-20px, labels 11-13px. Letter spacing 0. Body measure approximately 65 characters. Twelve-column desktop rhythm, 48-64px outside margins; 20px mobile margins and single-column figure flow. Rectangular media, no floating section cards. Tooltips for icon controls.

## 11. Motion

180-250ms link/thumbnail transitions, focus-visible equivalents, no scroll hijack, no content that requires animation to become readable, no autoplay external media, no custom cursor. Reduced motion removes nonessential transitions. Mobile behavior uses natural scroll and touch targets of at least 44px.

## 12-13. Reference principles, not copies

- [COLLINS / Bose](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/bose/): concise thesis and large media with deeper story on demand. Avoid hidden primary navigation and copying assets or rounded media frames.
- [Wolff Olins / Uber](https://www.wolffolins.com/work/uber): link business context, behavior and system change. Do not borrow corporate-scale outcome claims or turn the portfolio into a studio reel.
- [Koto / Amazon](https://koto.com/projects/amazon): modular chapters explain how a design system changes across use cases. Do not copy campaign typography, branded imagery or lengthy full-screen opening.
- [Porto Rocha / Vevo](https://www.portorocha.com/vevo): varying image scale and modular cadence are expressive while remaining systematic. Avoid its dense global project panel, automatic motion and proprietary identity.

## 14. Competitive value and honest limits

The differentiator is the continuity from published co-creation research to live spatial prototypes and production AI tools. The website should demonstrate polished interface craft while making who did what obvious. Source gaps remain: detailed Honda/Bubba original visuals, exact research findings and approved client-specific artifacts are not supplied. The architecture supports adding them; no false substitute is generated. The work can be strongly presented without claiming hiring outcomes.

## Concept-to-implementation decisions

- Kept the concept's large name, short positioning statement, quiet navigation, asymmetric project rhythm, dark research band and ruled editorial case structure.
- Rejected generated surgery dashboards, spatial renderings and authoring screens. These were visual composition studies, not Jumin's work. The shipped site uses original Ethicon and story-authoring imagery, and typographic scope/metric plates where original visuals are unavailable.
- Replaced invented experience and unsupported metrics in generated concepts with the supplied resume. Original conference work is co-authored; university-industry projects remain explicitly collaborations.
- Used a shorter real-image hero band and a compact-height desktop breakpoint so selected work remains visible at the fold. Mobile uses stacked identity and natural single-column reading instead of shrinking the desktop spread.
- Images reserve intrinsic dimensions and use manifest-derived responsive sizes. Actual interface artwork remains readable in full-size source links; decorative zoom is limited to thumbnail hover.
- Shared case structure provides consistent role, scope and outcome placement, while project-specific titles, artifact galleries, on-demand demos and retrospective diagrams vary the story. Resume-only work remains concise rather than padding gaps with fictional process.

The production build still targets GitHub Pages. Local verification can use `PORTFOLIO_BUILD_DIR` to leave tracked legacy `dist` artifacts untouched. Publishing requires a separate push/deployment; the redesign itself does not publish automatically.
